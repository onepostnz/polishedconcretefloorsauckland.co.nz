Commercial Polished Concrete

Polished concrete built for commercial demands

When you need commercial polished concrete in Auckland, you need a team that understands large-scale floors under constant traffic — not just a residential job scaled up.

Pro-Uplifters delivers commercial polished concrete for offices, retail units, warehouses, carparks and industrial buildings across Auckland. Whether it's a full grind and polish on a new slab, prep-grinding an existing floor, or floor coverings removal ahead of a fit-out, our team has the equipment and experience to handle commercial projects of any size.

What areas of Auckland do you cover?

We provide commercial polished concrete services right across the greater Auckland region for offices, warehouses, carparks and retail sites.

How quickly will I hear back after enquiring?

We aim to respond fast — our typical response time is around 75 seconds — so you can get moving on your project without delay.

Are you licensed and insured for commercial work?

Yes. Our team is fully insured and experienced in commercial and industrial polished concrete projects of all sizes.

Can you work around our business hours?

Yes. We plan commercial jobs around access, safety and minimal disruption to your operations, and can schedule work outside peak hours where needed.

Yes - floor coverings removal is one of our core services, so we can strip back tiles, carpet, vinyl or timber and prep the substrate before grinding and polishing.
