Carpet Tile Removal

Carpet tile removal without the mess

Carpet tiles are one of the most common commercial floor coverings in Auckland offices — and one of the trickiest to remove cleanly without the right equipment.

Pro-Uplifters specialises in carpet tile removal for offices, retail spaces and commercial buildings across Auckland. We lift the carpet tiles, then grind back the glued-down remnants and adhesive so the substrate is smooth, clean and ready for your next flooring installation.

Whether it's a small office refit or a full-floor commercial strip-out, our ride-on floor scraper and heavy duty equipment make short work of even heavily glued carpet tiles.

FAQs

Frequently asked questions

Common questions about our carpet tile removal enquiry service.

What areas of Auckland do you cover?

We provide carpet tile removal services right across the greater Auckland region for offices, retail and commercial sites.

Can you remove glued-down carpet tiles?

Yes — our ride-on floor scraper and heavy duty equipment are built for exactly this, including heavily glued commercial carpet tiles.

Will the adhesive residue be removed too?

Yes. Once the carpet tiles are lifted, we grind back the remaining adhesive so the substrate is smooth and ready for new flooring.

How quickly will I hear back after enquiring?

We aim to respond fast — our typical response time is around 75 seconds — so you can get moving on your project without delay.

Is this suitable for end of lease make-good work?

Absolutely. Carpet tile removal is one of the most common requirements for commercial tenants completing make-good obligations at lease end.
