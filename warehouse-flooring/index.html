Warehouse Flooring

Warehouse Flooring Auckland

In our line of business, we've found that polished concrete floors are as timeless as they are practical. At Pro-Uplifters, we provide polished concrete warehouse flooring services that are enduring, functional, and aesthetically pleasing.

Our process transforms ordinary, rough concrete surfaces into smooth, shiny floors that are not just visually appealing but also incredibly durable and easy to maintain - built to withstand heavy machinery and industrial wear.

The high-gloss finish reflects light beautifully, improving visibility and reducing the need for additional lighting, which can cut energy costs. They're also resistant to moisture and dust, promoting a healthier work environment.

What's included

  • Full inspection for cracks or damage, repaired before polishing
  • Heavy-duty grinding to reveal a smoother, uniform surface
  • Hardening agent applied to increase the floor's density
  • Finer grits used for the final high-gloss polish
  • Built to withstand heavy machinery and round-the-clock traffic
